Advanced Horse System

R6 mount, live hair physics, 3D customizer

anime styled horse riding system with 4 gears, drifting, power carving into turns, slope alignment, and a full 3d customizer where you can swap coats, mane and tail dye, saddle, bridle, all that. the mane and tail actually move with speed and wind in real time too.

lowky think the horse didnt even like me in the movement clip tho, didnt wanna jump when i told it to. maybe im just not a horse guy.

whats under the hood
  • 66 synced horse + R6 rider animations (walk, trot, canter, gallop, spur boost, drift, power carve, jump/fall, rear up, mount/dismount)
  • 4-gear momentum riding with terrain slope alignment, skid braking, and unmounted roaming/grazing AI
  • real-time spring-damper + wind physics on the mane and tail bones via Bone.Transform
  • in-game 3D customizer with 9 camera zones, 4 coats, bareback/saddle toggle, dyes, and live wind preview
  • custom aerodynamic wind trails, spur shockwave cone, hoof dust, and nostril breath vfx

Advanced Car System

2025 AMG G 63, raycast suspension, live crash damage

2 clips
Just driving around on the 60hz raycast suspension YouTube ↗
Crashing, opening doors, live dents & paint scratches YouTube ↗

2025 Mercedes-AMG G 63 running on custom 60hz raycast suspension instead of roblox wheel colliders, so it actually drives smooth and doesnt randomly bounce into orbit when you brush a curb. doors, hood, tailgate, mirrors, wipers, steering wheel, and brake calipers are all rigged up separately.

also built real-time 3d vertex crumpling and direct-on-paint scratches so smashing the front actually dents the front (took a minute to stop it from denting the back bumper instead lol). wreck it hard enough and it straight up totals.

whats under the hood
  • 60hz 4-corner raycast spring-damper suspension with server-pinned idle ownership so parked cars never jitter or flip
  • independently rigged doors, clamshell hood, tailgate, mirrors, wipers, coaxial steering wheel, and split wheel hubs/calipers
  • fuel system, headlights, turn signals, and self-lit OLED dash + cabin ambient lighting wired to ignition
  • directional 9-ray impact probing driving live EditableMesh vertex crumpling and EditableImage paint scratches
  • irreparable totaled state when you slam it hard enough
